Straight answers

The four questions everyone asks

Short versions here; each links to the full guide.

Do I need Japanese to work in Japan?

Depends on the role. Software engineering, design at international companies, and some finance run in English. Marketing, sales, support, and most domestic employers want N2 or better. The JLPT-by-role guide →

What visa do I need?

The default is Engineer / Specialist in Humanities — bachelor's degree or 10+ years of experience. Score 70+ HSP points and permanent residency fast-tracks. Run the eligibility checker →

Can I apply from abroad?

Yes — most large Japanese tech companies and foreign-capital Tokyo offices take overseas applications and sponsor visas plus relocation. {{ overseas_count }} indexed roles accept it right now. Filter the board →

How do I write a Japanese resume?

Japan expects two documents — the rirekisho (personal history) and shokumu keirekisho (career history) — and neither is a Western CV. The builders fill in English and export both. Open the builders →
